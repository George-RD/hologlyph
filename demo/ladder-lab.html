One backdrop, two rungs

The head over this hero is a transparent canvas. Rung 2 puts a backdrop-filter layer behind it, clipped to the silhouette, showing this page frosted and live. Rung 3 rasterises this same section into a texture the glass samples displaced by its own normals, so the page bends but is frozen between captures.

Both answer the same question, so only one of them is ever on screen: naming a source stands the frost down (dec.liquid-glass-rung-exclusion). Watch the stripe below. Inside the head it keeps sliding under rung 2 and freezes under rung 3.

Scroll past the hero and rung 3 keeps refracting it, because the snapshot is anchored in document space. Rung 2 has nothing to frost down here, which is the other half of the trade: the compositor only ever shows what is actually behind the canvas.
