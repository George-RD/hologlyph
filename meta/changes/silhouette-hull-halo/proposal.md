# Proposal: silhouette-hull-halo

## Motivation

The owner identified a halo around the gated compositor frost: its 32-point
convex hull reads as a separate patch behind the head.

## Scope

- Raise the primary silhouette-hull support directions to 32 and the point
  ceiling to 60.
- Rebuild the shipped GLB and re-verify its material audit.
- Record the performance, budget, provenance, and owner-judgement limits.

## Out of scope

- Enabling compositor frost by default.
- Adopting the unmeasured 252-point option.
- Replacing the convex hull with a concave outline.
