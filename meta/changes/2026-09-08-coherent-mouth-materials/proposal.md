# Coherent mouth materials

Teeth and tongue must remain clearly visible, with shading that belongs to the
text/glass head. A dark void and a luminous cavity both fail that goal.

The owner rejected the first dark material and subsequent coordinate-band
shaders. Preserve the real source identities instead: teeth from ICT's material
group, tongue from the authored mask, and gums as dark negative space. Share the
head's live glyph and deformation graphs without its transparency.

Scope: mouth shading, a source-derived region attribute on the shipped GLB,
reproducibility tests and real-engine screenshots. No speech retuning, new public
controls or live-demo deployment. This supersedes the initial dark-mouth brief.
